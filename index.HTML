OUR STORY

Crafted with Passion Since 1981

Founded in 1981, Girard Café is one of the UAE's earliest French coffee and pastry houses, rooted in tradition and shaped by decades of craftsmanship.

Established by Girard Raymond from the south of France, the café brought authentic French coffee culture to the Emirates — creating a timeless place where passion lives in every cup.
